🎯 Module 2 • Examination Format & Criteria

The MTF ICAO Level Test: Overview & Rules

🎖️

Official Civil Aviation Language Certification

Certifies students at ICAO Level 4 (Operational Level) or Level 5 (Extended Level).

⚠️

The ICAO "All-or-Nothing" Rule

You must obtain at least Level 4 in ALL SIX ICAO criteria (no averaging allowed):

1. Pronunciation 2. Structure 3. Vocabulary 4. Fluency 5. Comprehension 6. Interactions
⏱️

30-Minute Test with Independent Remote Rating

The interlocutor facilitates communication and does NOT assess. Audio is rated blind by 2 remote raters (+ 3rd arbitrator if split).

📸 🎧 📰 The Three Exam Tasks

The Three MTF Examination Tasks

TASK 1 • ~10 MINS 1 Minute Preparation

Visual Input: Aeronautical Picture Description

Describe an aeronautical photograph (aircraft, ramp vehicles, personnel, runway layout). Identify hazards, explain what is happening, and speculate on causes and operational consequences.

TASK 2 • ~10 MINS Live Traffic Radio Audio

Audio Input: Seven Pilot & Controller Messages

Listen to 7 pre-recorded authentic communications. Identify the aircraft action, pilot problem, or clearance across flight phases (pre-flight, takeoff, en route, approach).

TASK 3 • ~10 MINS 5 Minutes Preparation

Textual Input: Aviation News Text Summary & Q&A

Read a 200-word aeronautical news article. Deliver a spontaneous spoken summary with paraphrasing, discuss 3 suggested gap-fill options, and engage in open technical debate.

💡 Proven Techniques • Level 4 / 5 Mastery

MTF Strategies for High-Scoring Success

🖼️

Picture Strategy: The 4-Step Narrative

Overview → Key Elements (foreground/background) → Past Causes (must have) → Future Impact (might require).

📻

Live Traffic Rule: Catch Information in the Readback

Controllers issue clearances, pilots read them back—you hear numbers and clearances twice! Never leave blanks on your candidate sheet.

✍️

Text Summary: The 100% Synonym Rule

Only swap words if you are 100% certain of the meaning; otherwise change sentence syntax (active to passive). Speak from notes—never read sentences verbatim.
