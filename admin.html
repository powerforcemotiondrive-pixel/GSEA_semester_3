Dynamic Formative Evaluation (FE) Rotations:

To ensure variety in group work and timeslot fairness, groups do not have fixed pairs or trios. Each group rotates across 4 slots of 30 minutes (2 hours total). Every student experiences different peers and rotates between early and late slots across each Formative Evaluation. Switch to the FE Tutorial Rotas tab to view your complete evaluation schedule.

Official Formative Evaluation Rota • GSEA 25A Dynamic Rotations • 4 Slots of 30 min (2 Hours)

30-Minute Oral Tutorial Timetables

Every student group has an exact 30-minute appointment slot with their assigned teacher. To guarantee fairness, pairings and trios rotate across each Formative Evaluation, alternating early and late slots across all groups.

Essential Checklist for Formative Evaluation Day

1. Arrive Early

Be outside the examination room 5 minutes before your scheduled start time.

2. Visual Supports

Have your slides or handouts ready to display immediately. Do not read from notes!

3. Technical Vocabulary & Portfolio

Have your technical vocabulary logs and portfolio reflection entries open and ready for inspection.

4. Self-Critique Ready

Arrive with your personal strong points and weak points clearly thought through to discuss.
