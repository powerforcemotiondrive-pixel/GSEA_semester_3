30-Minute Oral Tutorial Timetables

Every student group has an exact 30-minute appointment slot with their assigned teacher. To guarantee fairness, pairings and trios rotate across each Formative Evaluation, alternating early and late slots across all groups.

Essential Checklist for Formative Evaluation Day

1. Arrive Early

Be outside the examination room 5 minutes before your scheduled start time.

2. Visual Supports

Have your slides or handouts ready to display immediately. Do not read from notes!

3. Vocabulary & TOEIC

Have your vocabulary logs and completed TOEIC practice records open and ready for inspection.

4. Self-Critique Ready

Arrive with your personal strong points and weak points clearly thought through to discuss.
